## Standing constraints

- The 30-day derived-data retention and 24h/7d raw-document defaults remain in
  force until a new policy version is approved and accepted.
- No unreviewed rule or benchmark may be activated to satisfy a commercial date.
- No decision may enable “illegal” conclusions, outcome prediction, or tax advice.
